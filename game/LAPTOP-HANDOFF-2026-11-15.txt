USER INTENT
===========
Miso is a cute adult ginger tabby companion for the Folio reader app. The cat
window should occupy roughly the upper half to two-thirds of the screen when
this submode is open. The user's target phone is a Galaxy S10; fit the actual
container/viewport, rather than hard-coding a hardware pixel width.

Keep the scope modest: one fixed isometric room, addable decorations and useful
cat interactions. Future wishes include a small garden, gradual room expansion
or a few rooms, and fixed unlockable furnishings, reminiscent of a small town
opening up over time. Garden/expansion/economy/unlocks are NOT implemented yet.
Do not turn this into a large crafting/base-management game.

The user rejected the early script-drawn cats, inconsistent chunky silhouettes,
box-like legs and extra paws. The established workflow uses image generation
conditioned on frozen master sprites, complete poses, fixed grids, native export
and separate art/motion review. Never regenerate the character from scratch for
each animation, or use the latest generated frame as a new identity master.

LATEST EXPLICIT DECISIONS
=========================
- Native Miso source: 64x64, root [31,55], frozen 16-color palette.
- Display Miso uniformly at 1.30x: the user asked for 30% bigger after v2 felt
  too small. Do not resize/repaint the master PNG to achieve this.
- Environment is larger-format: 128x64 projected floor diamonds, 320x384 padded
  prop cells. The scene is five by five logical floor squares, fixed 2:1 camera.
- Soft environment scaling/edges are intentional. Miso sampling stays crisp.
- Floor -> lower step -> top perch -> book-cubby roof; reverse safely to leave.
- Top perch has watch/groom/nap; bookshelf has a nap. Wake before moving.
- Window has a broad padded landing ledge, not just a thin drawn frame.
- Food and water have separate front/back approaches and matching poses.
- Tunnel has dedicated duck/crawl/emerge art and correct interior/mouth masking.
- Layering uses pixel/height-aware occlusion, not whole-object centre sorting.

CURRENT CAPABILITIES
====================
21 activities, five movement speeds, all-direction turns, jump arcs, feeding,
scratching, low tunnel crawl, box hiding, sofa/rug naps, elevated naps/grooming,
window watching, closed-door investigation, feather pounce and toy nudge.
Small leaf/vine and hanging-ball pivots, water ripples and lamp glow animate
without wobbling whole pieces of furniture. Ambient motion can be disabled;
reduced-motion preferences are respected. Optional automatic exploration works.

There are 289 native cat atlas frames and 34 clip definitions. The 277 frames
and 29 definitions from before the last review are unchanged. The last review
added twelve complete cat drawings / five feeding-and-tunnel clips, plus edited
window and tower fixtures. Approved run_sw, sprint_sw and sprint_nw art, phase
order and timing remain protected unless the user explicitly asks to change them.

The room layout is currently fixed. The old modular placement editor is a
separate earlier prototype; the current playable page does not contain it.
The high plant shelf is decorative. The "bookshelf" landing is the low wall
book cubby's roof. The ball toy nudges; it has no independently orbiting ball.
